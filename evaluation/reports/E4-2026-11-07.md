## 2. Findings
**MUST**: none in Phase 6 scope.

**SHOULD**
- S1 `packages/engines/src/credassure/engines/normaliser.py:124` and `:143`: the strict `parse_money` and `parse_rate`
  reject the common suffixes `/-` and `p.a.`. `routers/cases.py` `_cell` uses `parse_money` for uploaded CSV money
  cells, so `1,42,00,000/-` fails validation (closed, with a field error). **Fix:** strip a trailing `/-` and
  `p.a.`/`per annum` before matching, and add both to the 50+ suite.
- S2 Critic VER-02..05 and #21 are not built. **Fix:** Phase 9d, then re-run the Critic half of E4.

**NIT**
- N1 `packages/security/src/credassure/security/pii.py` PHONE: a bare 12-digit number `91[6-9]…` is redacted as a
  phone (90/20,000 random numbers). This is a real +91 mobile format; amounts that large in plain digits are
  implausible. **Fix:** none; already documented as a ceiling.
- N2 RT14 (tool/secret probing) is not an injection hit. **Fix:** cover it in the copilot refusal policy (9e,
  `system_prompt_leak`), not in the scanner.
