## (2) Findings

**MUST**
- None for Phase 7 scope. The KR-05 target is a gate shortfall (G2), not a code defect. It is recorded OPEN under SHOULD 1 and 2.

**SHOULD**
1. **Labels are too narrow for the grown corpus** (`evaluation/src/credassure/evaluation/build_assets.py` queries). Each query names 1–2 documents, but the corpus now has 161 CGTMSE circulars, 101 Parliament answers and 94 GST circulars. For 8 of the 10 misses examined, no labelled document is in the top 10 because other documents outrank it, and some of those plausibly answer the question. For example, Q01's top hit is FAQ-020, which states the R-CG-02 rule.
   - Fix: a non-copilot owner pools the top 10 results across the 18 configurations and judges them, then adds the relevant documents to `relevant_doc_ids`.
2. **7 queries cannot be answered yet** (Q11, Q12, Q26, Q30, Q31, Q36, Q37), so the ceiling is 33/40 even with perfect retrieval.
   - Fix: deliver R-MSME-01, R-JUD-001, R-BANK-03/04/05, R-RBI-11 and R-RBI-01 from the corpus worktree.
3. **All 111 case-scoped KFS documents have effective_from 2026-11-16, one day after AS_OF_DATE (2026-11-15)** (`data/frozen/v1/kfs/*.md`). Under KR-04 none of them can be retrieved on the as-of date, so copilot KFS questions in 9e will fail and LW-04 must not read KFS through search.
   - Fix (owner decision): date the KFS on or before the offer date ≤ AS_OF in a v2 data set, or exempt case-scoped documents from the date filter by ADR. Do not change frozen data in place.
4. **R-RBI-06 (PSL Directions) is a captured page shell.** Its text has no "priority sector", so Q23 depends on R-RBI-FAQ-001 alone.
   - Fix: re-save the PDF by hand, as was done for R-RBI-02 and R-RBI-10.
5. **Noisy PDF headings produce odd section paths** (Q13's label is "5. Hindu undivided"; Gazette banners) (`chunking.py` `_heading`).
   - Fix: when labels are revisited, consider a minimum body length per section, or treat headings with no body as running text.

**NIT**
- Q35's top passage is the Act's "Schedule." section (amendments), not penalties. The cross-encoder favours long Gazette chunks. Watch this when choosing the demo default.
- The best configuration is tied (section/dense/rerank and section/hybrid/off are both 23/40). ADR 0013 says to choose the better permitted setting. Hybrid without the reranker is just as good and cheaper, so keep `RERANKER=none` until labels are fixed and the comparison is re-run.

## (3) Verdict

**NOT READY for the G2 retrieval target (KR-05 23/40 < 34).** The Phase 7 retrieval pipeline itself is complete and correct: every KR-01..04 check passes, the KR-04 filters have 0 violations, and isolation holds.

The three fixes with the most impact:
1. Widen the retrieval labels by judging pooled results (SHOULD 1).
2. Deliver the 7 missing answer documents and re-save R-RBI-06 (SHOULD 2 and 4).
3. Resolve the KFS date versus AS_OF_DATE conflict before Phase 9e (SHOULD 3).
