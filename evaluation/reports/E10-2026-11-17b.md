## 2. Findings
- **SHOULD 1** — `apps/gateway/tests/test_phase10_contract.py:128`: the fuzz DB has no case, so case-scoped routes stop at 404 and the decision logic is never fuzzed past it. The VP role is not fuzzed. **Fix:** seed one AWAITING_APPROVAL case, pin `case_id` with a schemathesis hook, and add `demo.vp`.
- **SHOULD 2** — `.githooks/pre-push:2`: #28 and #51 are enforced only by a local hook (CI removed in cd57a3e), and no ADR records this. **Fix:** an ADR amending #28 and #51. *(Done after this report: ADR 0027.)*
- **NIT 1** — `core/budget.py:42` with `llm/client.py`: a call that times out after the server spent tokens is charged 0. The 10-step cap still bounds it. **Fix:** charge the estimate on exception.
- **NIT 2 (carried)** — `main.py:624`: CORS lacks DELETE.
- **NIT 3 (carried)** — `settings.py:89`: `approval_threshold_paise` is unused.
- **NIT 4 (carried)** — `workflow.py:257,267`: the step-up limiter `test` then `hit` is not atomic.

## 3. Verdict: **READY**
1. Seed a case in the fuzz fixture and add the VP role.
2. Record CI → pre-push in an ADR (done: ADR 0027).
3. Charge the reserved estimate when a model call fails.
