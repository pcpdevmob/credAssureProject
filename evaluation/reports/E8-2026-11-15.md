**Read with care.**
- **Pooling bias:** silver labels were judged only on what these 18 configurations returned in their top 5, so recall@5
  on them is optimistic by construction. They are good for comparing configurations, not for an absolute claim.
- **Self-consistency is an upper bound:** each duplicate is identical text in the same query group, so 100% shows a
  stable judge, not a correct one.
- A silver label on a passage before any heading is `exact` (only unsectioned passages match), so one preamble chunk
  does not label every section of R-RATE-02-BOB, R-RBI-06 or R-RBI-FAQ-003.

**KR-05 stays OPEN** until human agreement is in: `make judge JUDGE=pritish` on the 249-item sample (stratified by

## Addendum 3 — 2026-11-15 (exit policy, weighted kappa)

- **`make retrieval-eval` exit code:** fails (1) only on a KR-04 violation or an isolation **leak** (a top-10 passage
  that fails KR-04 for a case-tied check, another case's KFS above all). An own-KFS **recall miss** is reported in the
  isolation table as `RECALL MISS` and does not fail the command. The known ISO3 `semantic/dense/rerank=off` miss
  (own KFS 17/18) is therefore reported, not failing; leakage stays 54/54 clean. Tests: `test_KR04_violation_fails_the_command`,
  `test_SUP08_isolation_leak_fails_the_command`, `test_SUP08_own_kfs_recall_miss_is_reported_but_passes`.
- **`make kappa`:** prints plain Cohen's κ on the judged sample, plus agreement and κ weighted back to the full pool's
  silver-grade mix (each item weighs N_g / n_g, its grade's pool count over its judged count). The ≥ 0.7 verdict uses
  the **weighted κ**. The sample is proportionally stratified, so the two should be close; they diverge only if the
  sample is incomplete or a stratum is judged unevenly.
- **Re-run after the G1 label pass (B1, same day; `retrieval-2026-11-15.md` regenerated):** exit 0; KR-04 violations 0;
  isolation leaks 0, recall misses 1 (ISO3 `semantic/dense/rerank=off`). Every label now names a section (or is an
  explicit `exact` whole document for headingless files), so document-level hits no longer count. Best recall@5:
  v1.0 **24/40** (was 26), v1.1-silver strict **35/40** (was 37), lenient **38/40** (unchanged). The drop is the
