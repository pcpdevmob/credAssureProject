| Gold rank | Items |
|---|---|
| Top 5 | T003, T007, T008, T010, T011, T012, T014, T015, T017, T016 |
| 6–30 | T001 (18), T002 (8), T009 |
| Not in top 30 | T004, T005, T006, T013, T018, T019 |

- **Ceiling:** about 16/25 (10 reachable plus 6 abstentions) even with a perfect answerer. This is the open KR-05 gap (26/40 recall@5 against a target of 34).
- **Reranker sensitivity:** with `local_cross_encoder`, 8 of 19 items have gold in the top 5, so the reranker stays off (ADR 0013).

## (5) Findings

**MUST**
- **M1 (fixed):** the Critic's grounded-wording rule let approval predictions through when a word pair such as "approved by" appeared in a cited passage (security review). Now no banned rule is excused, except quoted noun terms ("guaranteed/sanctioned amount|portion|limit|cover") found in a cited passage. Regression test: `test_VER03_prediction_whose_word_pair_is_in_the_passage_is_still_blocked`.
- **M2 (OPEN):** CP-01 ≥20/25 is not met (live 10/25; ceiling about 16 while KR-05 is open). Fix path: (a) human-checked v1.1 retrieval labels and a re-measure; (b) tag outdated limits documents (R-SIDBI-P-008, R-CG-C-274 and peers) with `superseded_by`; (c) only then reconsider ≤2 regenerations of `cp_answer`.

**SHOULD**
- **S1 (fixed):** history turns flagged by the injection scanner are no longer replayed to the model.
- **S2 (fixed):** refusal now also catches paraphrased approval odds and acceptance ("going to approve", "accept Lender B").
- **S3 (fixed):** a search tool error (embedder or Chroma down) returns the abstention instead of HTTP 500.
- **S4 (open):** the own-KFS clause matches `doc_id@sha256` without a tenant term. Exploiting it needs the exact bytes, which are frozen synthetic files. Add a tenant term when the corpus gains per-tenant KFS.
- **S5 (open):** the public search uses the default `lender_type=commercial_bank`. An NBFC case would get bank-only public rules (own KFS are exempt).
- **S6 (open, Phase 11):** ablations A3 and A5 do not reach the copilot path yet.

**NIT**
- The embedder is built from `os.environ`, not from Settings. Keyword mode does not pass `tenant_id` (harmless today).
