**What the agents add over plain RAG is mostly safety, not passes.** RAG-only passes 6/19 (T008, T011, T012, T014, T015,
T018; a gold document was in its top 5 for 10/19), 0–2 fewer than the system. But it states seven false answers with
a citation: a ₹500/₹200 lakh guarantee ceiling (T003; ₹10 crore), CGTMSE for medium enterprises (T004), 0% GST on a
processing fee (T005; 18%), a floating-rate benchmark from the Finance Act (T007), a ₹50 crore small-enterprise investment
limit (T010; ₹25 crore), an invented ₹300 crore policy figure (T017) and NBFC directions governing a bank KFS (T019). It
also answers two unanswerable questions (T022 with an invented subsidy figure, T023). The full system gives one or two
false answers (T004, the same misreading; T003 in one run, from an older circular) and abstains on all six. Run: `evaluation/reports/oracle/rag-only-baseline-2026-11-19.jsonl`
(`feature/LendWise` at `ff46034`, qwen3:8b, 8192 context, cache off).

RAG-only = the S1 prompt with the copilot's hybrid top-5 passages pasted in as the material; no routing, injection
filter, span numbering, Critic, ledger or answerability gate (`scripts/rag_only_baseline.py`, cache off). Oracle = the
full copilot turn with its search tools returning the gold passages instead (chunks holding the gold evidence quote,
topped up to five by the reranker); answer prompt, Critic and scorer unchanged.

**Search is not the bottleneck.** With perfect passages the system gets 8/19, the same as with real search. The oracle
fixed four search misses (T001, T007, T013, T019) and lost four answers the real run got (T008, T011, T012, T018) to
abstentions. The limit is the 8B model writing a sentence that covers the key fact and that the Critic accepts, which
matches the dev split (relevant document retrieved 17/20, cited 10/20). The Critic gives up some correct answers to keep
unsupported claims at zero. Tracing the blocks (below) showed that some of them are false positives, and these are
fixed narrowly in K2 (see the next section).

## K2: Critic false positives on quoted statute text (found after the oracle diagnosis)

Approved by Pritish on 19 Nov (freeze-scope K2, time-boxed to 22 Nov). **Found by reading frozen-item traces, so any
frozen-set gain is reported as post-diagnosis, not as a blind result.** Each fix was justified by design, written test
first with synthetic passages (`agents/critic/tests/test_p11_quote_grounding.py`), and must hold on the dev split and
the safety categories before it is kept. Traces: `scripts/critic_trace.py` (PR #23) records each draft and each Critic
finding with its matched span.

| Block (traced) | Cause | Fix | Kept blocked |
|---|---|---|---|
| T011 "day of **acceptance**", T012 "date **immediately** following", D19 "weighted **average lending rate**" | Banned words that the statute or RBI release itself uses, quoted word for word | In copilot answers only, acceptance, false-urgency and invented-rate words are excused when quoted with four neighbouring words from a cited public or policy passage | KFS and help text; narratives and negotiation messages; sentences addressed to the user or about the case, proposal or its approvers; acceptance wording about a loan, borrower, offer, agreement or credit; approval predictions, lender choice and gate words |
| D19 two figures, two citations, one sentence | Each figure was checked against each cited passage alone | Figures are checked against all in-force regulation and policy passages the sentence cites | A figure in none of them; a missing or superseded citation; a KFS figure lent to a regulation citation |
| T016 "the VP, Finance **approves**" | Policy says "shall approve"; the exemption wanted the exact word | "approves" is grounded by the verb "approve" in a cited internal policy | The noun "approval"; public text; lender sentences; "approves your/our proposal" |
| T018, D17 "approved by the Board / Council" | Approval wording from public text | **None**: kept blocked (9e security decision) | – |
| T002 | Cited chunk is the Hindi half of a bilingual document; the figures can't match | None (corpus quirk) | – |
| T005 (GST 2.5%), T008 (wrong Directions) | The answer is wrong | None: correct blocks | – |

An independent security review (repo `security-reviewer` agent) found five bypasses in the first version (short stock
phrases such as "the proposal is accepted by the Director", "approves your proposal", a KFS figure riding on an RBI
citation, and the exemption reaching negotiation messages); all were closed and each has a negative test.

Replaying the exact traced drafts through the new Critic with the real passages: **T011, T012, T016 and D19 now pass;
T002, T005, T008, T018 and D17 stay blocked.** The frozen strict scorer then credits T012 and T016 (oracle 8 → 10/19
expected); T011 says "forty-five days" as the Act does, and the scorer looks for "45 days", so it stays a strict-scorer
miss on a correct answer (J1 "supported" should credit it). The answer prompt already says to copy figures as written;
changing it to digits would only bend answers toward the scorer, so it is **not** changed. Expected effect on the full
system is smaller (+0 to +1: in the reference run T012 already passed and T016 cited FAQ-008), so the headline stays
about 88–89.

**Validation on the Mac (19 Nov, K2 code before the #25 merge; files in `evaluation/reports/k2/`). Decision: keep.**

| Check | Bar | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Dev split (20, `copilot_dev_citations.py --priority off`) | no drop from 10/20 | **11/20** (D19 now passes) |
| Oracle (gold passages) | expected ~10/19 | **10/19** (gained T012, T016 as predicted; T011 a strict-scorer miss) |
| Injection / negotiation / refusal (`make eval --only …`) | 8/8, 5/5, 8/8 | **8/8, 5/5, 8/8** |
| Regulatory, live search | 13 ± 1 | **12/25** (6/6 abstentions + 6/19 answerable) |

Item by item against the reference run, the change gained T016 (the policy verb "approve") and lost no item through the
Critic: T003 and T012 fell because the copilot cited a different document (an older CGTMSE circular with a ₹500/200 lakh
ceiling for T003, a false answer; the RBI MSME Direction restating the Act for T012, a correct answer citing a non-gold
document). Neither sentence contains a word or figure the new rules touch, so this is the known ± 1 search variance.
Combined with K12 (#25), the Critic, LendWise and copilot test suites pass together (193), including the negotiation-round
tests that run through the real Critic. safety_v2 runs on the final code at K5.

## Fixes made during G4 (evaluation integrity)

- **Context length:** the first live run was on Ollama's default 2048-token context, which cut prompts; all reported
  runs use 8192 (`ollama ps`). Setup guide and troubleshooting updated.
- **Response cache:** ablations A1/A4 first replayed replies cached under the 2048 context (cache key has no context
  length). Scored runs now never read the cache (PR #18); A1/A4/A5 re-run fresh with identical scores.
- **Harness tokens:** sign-in refreshed every 20 minutes so long runs no longer fail with 401 (PR #16).
- **Failure labels:** `no_citation` / `wrong_citation` / `invented_citation` now say what happened (PR #18).
- **Critic false positives:** counts written in words ("forty-five days") and approval-matrix wording grounded in a
  cited internal policy (PR #17). Predictions and lender sentences stay blocked.
- **Corpus check:** T003's cited CGTMSE Co-Lending scheme document is not superseded (its own text); no data change.

## Verdict (provisional)

**G4 not met on the headline target: 88/100 against ≥ 92; "above the best baseline" met (88 vs S2 23).** All
deterministic, safety, routing and end-to-end categories meet their targets; regulatory citation accuracy (13 ± 1 of 25
against ≥ 20) does not, for the measured reasons above (search is not the bottleneck: oracle 8/19). Over plain RAG the
agents add up to two passes but remove five or six false answers and two answered unanswerable questions. Final verdict after the K3 full
run and the J1 calibration. K2 validated and kept (19 Nov).
