# Approver

## Inbox
**Approvals** lists the proposals routed to your role. A validated need above ₹1,50,00,000 goes to the VP, Finance and
one below it goes to the Director, Finance. Exactly ₹1,50,00,000 is held for a human decision.

## Decision
Open the case on **Review & approval**. Choose approve, reject, or return to a named stage. A comment is required to
reject or return. The button opens a confirmation: type the case ID to confirm. The decision is recorded with your name
and time and cannot be edited. **Approval is internal, not loan acceptance.** The MSME chooses its lender outside
CredAssure. You cannot approve a case you prepared.
