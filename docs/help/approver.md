# Approver

## Inbox
**Approvals** lists the proposals routed to your role. A validated need above ₹1,50,00,000 goes to the VP, Finance and
one below it goes to the Director, Finance. Exactly ₹1,50,00,000 is held for a human decision.

A case held at exactly ₹1,50,00,000 is marked **HELD**. The inbox shows only what the service returns for your role.

## Decision
Open the case on **Review & approval**. Read the **Safety events** panel first: it lists what the guardrails and the
Verification Critic blocked, redacted or flagged on this case (codes only, never the text). To approve, tick *I reviewed
the Critic findings and the safety events*; the approval records that you did. Text marked **AI-generated draft** was
written by the language model and checked by the Critic; every figure in it comes from the fact ledger. Choose approve, reject, or return to a named stage. A comment is required to
reject or return. The button opens a confirmation: type the case ID to confirm. The decision is recorded with your name
and time and cannot be edited. **Approval is internal, not loan acceptance.** The MSME chooses its lender outside
CredAssure. You cannot approve a case you prepared.

If the service refuses your decision, the screen says why: the person who prepared a proposal cannot approve it, and
only the routed approver can decide. **Report** shows the proposal report and every figure it rests on; open a figure to
see its source. Export it as Markdown or HTML.
