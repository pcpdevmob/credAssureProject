# FAQ

**Why isn't the loan amount the ₹2 crore we asked for?**
The requested amount is only an input. The funding need is calculated from the weekly cash flow: peak shortfall plus
the minimum cash buffer, less undrawn credit lines and confirmed mitigations, rounded up to the next lakh.

**Why is the cheapest-looking lender not ranked first?**
Offers are ranked by APR, which includes fees, GST on fees and any guarantee fee. An offer whose instalments would push
cash below the buffer is marked unaffordable and never ranked first.

**Did the AI decide this?**
No. Gates, figures, ranking and routing are decided by code. Text marked **AI-generated draft** was written by the
language model and checked by the Verification Critic. A person makes the decision.

**Does approval accept the loan?**
No. **Approval is internal, not loan acceptance.** The MSME chooses its lender outside CredAssure.

**Why did Ask CredAssure say "I can't verify that from the available sources"?**
It answers only from cited passages and this case's verified figures. When nothing it found supports an answer, it
says so instead of guessing.

**Can I type an Aadhaar, PAN or account number into the chat?**
Please don't. They are removed before the question is stored or sent to the model.

**Why can't I approve this case?**
Only the routed approver can decide, and the person who prepared a case can never approve it. If the report changed
since you opened it, reload and read the new version first.
